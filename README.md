## 🗂️ What Moobot Desktop Actually Does

Moobot stores its settings in a folder on your computer, and it stores exported data in another. Those folders change over time — you tweak a setting, you export a fresh file, and the old version quietly disappears.

Moobot Desktop solves that problem. Before a patch or update touches your data, the utility makes a dated copy of your config files and export files and keeps those copies safely on disk. The date is part of the folder or file name, so you can tell at a glance which copy came from which day.

Think of it like a photo album for your settings. Nothing is deleted, nothing is overwritten, and you can always flip back to a page from last week.

### Why dated copies matter

- **Patches can change formats.** An update might rearrange how settings are stored. A dated copy from before the patch still opens the old way.
- **Mistakes happen.** A wrong toggle or an accidental reset is far less painful when yesterday's config is sitting right there.
- **Exports are snapshots.** Keeping dated export files means you can compare what your data looked like at two different points in time.
- **Peace of mind.** Knowing a backup exists makes it much easier to try new things.
